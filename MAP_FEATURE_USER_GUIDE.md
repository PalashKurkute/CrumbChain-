# Map Feature - User Guide

## For Receivers

### Accessing the Map

1. **Login** to the CrumbChain app as a Receiver
2. On the **Receiver Home Page**, you'll see four feature cards
3. Tap on the **"Explore Listings"** card (second card, top right)
4. The map page will open

### Understanding the Map Interface

#### Map Elements:

**Top Bar:**
- Title: "Available Listings"
- Back button (←) - Return to home page
- My Location button (🎯) - Centers map on your current location
- Refresh button (🔄) - Reloads listings from server

**Map View:**
- Blue circular marker = Your current location (if permission granted)
- Orange pin markers (📍) = Available food listings
- Restaurant icon (🍽️) on each pin = Food donation location

**Badge (Top Left):**
- Shows total number of available listings
- Example: "🍽️ 6 available"

### Using the Map

#### Navigation:
- **Pan**: Drag with one finger to move around the map
- **Zoom**: Pinch with two fingers or use +/- buttons
- **Rotate**: Rotate with two fingers (optional)

#### Viewing Listings:
1. **Tap any orange marker** to view listing details
2. A bottom sheet will slide up showing:
   - Food type (title)
   - Donor name
   - Description
   - Quantity available
   - Dietary tag (Vegetarian/Non-Vegetarian/Vegan)
   - Temperature status
   - Packaging type
   - Location address
   - Pickup time
   - Date prepared
   - Payment info (if applicable)

#### Actions on Listings:
- **Claim Food**: Reserve the listing (coming soon)
- **Directions**: Get navigation to pickup location (coming soon)

### Permission Requests

When you first open the map, the app may ask for:

**Location Permission:**
- **Allow**: Your location will be shown on the map
- **Deny**: Map still works, but you won't see your location marker

You can change this later in your device settings.

### Tips for Best Experience

1. **Grant Location Permission**: Helps you see how far listings are from you
2. **Use Refresh Button**: Check for new listings regularly
3. **Tap Multiple Markers**: Compare different listings before deciding
4. **Check Pickup Times**: Make sure you can pick up during the specified time
5. **Note the Distance**: Closer listings are easier to reach

### Common Scenarios

#### Scenario 1: Finding Food Near You
1. Open the map
2. Tap "My Location" button to center on your position
3. Look at nearby orange markers
4. Tap markers to compare options
5. Choose based on food type, quantity, and pickup time

#### Scenario 2: Searching in Different Areas
1. Pan the map to explore different neighborhoods
2. Zoom out to see a wider area
3. Zoom in on interesting clusters of markers
4. Tap markers to view details

#### Scenario 3: Refreshing Listings
1. Tap the refresh button (top right)
2. Wait for new listings to load
3. New markers will appear, old ones may disappear

### Understanding Listing Information

**Dietary Tags:**
- 🥬 **Vegetarian**: No meat, but may contain dairy/eggs
- 🍖 **Non-Vegetarian**: Contains meat/fish
- 🌱 **Vegan**: No animal products

**Temperature Status:**
- 🔥 **Hot**: Freshly cooked, consume within 2 hours
- ❄️ **Cold**: Refrigerated items
- 🌡️ **Room Temperature**: Shelf-stable items

**Packaging Types:**
- Sealed Containers
- Cardboard Boxes
- Plastic Wrap
- Reusable Containers

**Status Colors:**
- Orange marker = Active (available)
- (Future: Different colors for claimed/completed)

### What to Do After Finding a Listing

1. **Note the Details**: Save or remember key information
2. **Check Pickup Time**: Ensure you can arrive during the window
3. **Plan Your Route**: Use external map apps for now
4. **Contact Donor**: Through app messaging (future feature)
5. **Claim the Listing**: Use claim button (coming soon)

### Troubleshooting

**Problem**: Map is blank/white
- **Solution**: Check internet connection, wait a moment for tiles to load

**Problem**: No markers visible
- **Solution**: Zoom out or tap refresh button

**Problem**: Can't see my location
- **Solution**: Grant location permission in settings, tap "My Location" button

**Problem**: Listing details won't open
- **Solution**: Ensure you're tapping directly on the marker pin

**Problem**: Old listings still showing
- **Solution**: Tap refresh button to reload

### Privacy & Safety

- Your location is only used to show your position on the map
- Your location is NOT shared with donors or stored on servers
- All listings are from verified donors
- Meet in public places or designated pickup points
- Follow food safety guidelines

### Best Practices

1. **Plan Ahead**: Check pickup times before traveling
2. **Be Punctual**: Arrive during specified pickup window
3. **Bring Containers**: Some listings may need your own containers
4. **Check Distance**: Consider travel time and food freshness
5. **Coordinate**: Contact donor if you'll be late (future feature)

## For Donors

### Your Listings on the Map

When you create a listing:
- It automatically appears on the receivers' map
- The location you entered is geocoded to coordinates
- An orange marker is placed at your location
- Receivers can tap the marker to see full details

### Creating Map-Friendly Listings

**Location Field:**
- Be specific and complete
- Format: "Area, City, State, Country"
- Example: "Andheri West, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India"
- Avoid abbreviations

**Good Location Examples:**
- ✅ "Bandra West, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India"
- ✅ "Powai, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India"
- ✅ "Colaba, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India"

**Bad Location Examples:**
- ❌ "Mumbai" (too vague)
- ❌ "Near railway station" (not specific)
- ❌ "My house" (not geocodable)

### Checking Your Listings

Currently, donors don't have access to the map view, but your listings are visible to all receivers on their map.
